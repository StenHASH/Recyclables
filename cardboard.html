SECTION 2 - QUICK ALTERNATIVE

When You're On The Go

On the move? You can still make a responsible disposal decision in a few quick steps.

1

Inspect & Flatten

Check that your box is not soaked or soiled.

Flatten it my stepping on it on dry ground and make sure its as compact as possible.

If it is wet or stained, feel free to throw it in the trash.

2

Look Around For A Paper Recycling Bin

Take a look around for a Paper Recycling Bin. If you spot one, go ahead and recycle your box!

If it cannot fit, try and fold it once more or leave it on the side.

3

Hold Onto It Or Not?

If you can't immediately find a recycling bin, decide if the flattened cardboard is light and compact enough for you to hold onto.

If you can, keep it on you and carry on. Keep an eye out for a recycling bin on your activities to drop it off at.

If it is too much of a hassle, find the nearest trash bin and dispose of it there.

Important: You do not have to remove tape or labels. Dented, ripped and damage cardboard are also able to be recycled!

Source: American Forest & Paper Association - How To Recycle Your Cardboard Boxes

SECTION 4 - REDUCE

Use Fewer Cardboard Boxes

A few less boxes make much less hassle.

1

Combine Online Orders

Most cardboard boxes come from online deliveries. If you are buying from the same seller, make sure you have all items you want in a single order before checkout!

The seller would be as glad to pack all your items into one single cardboard box. Less work for everyone!

2

Reuse It At Home

If its in good condition, reuse it to store items away or for moving them around!

One box reused is a box you never had to buy new!

Easy Recycling: Cardboard is key in everyday packaging and is super easy to recycle. Make sure you recycle it everytime!

Source: Business Waste - How To Reduce Cardboard Waste

SECTION 5 - REUSE

Use It Another Way

A cardboard box makes good temporary storage too!

1

Reuse Your Boxes For Storage

Cardboard is versatile & sturdy, great as temporary storage boxes within your home.

Try using it to hold trash bags as an outer lining. It will soak up spills but do throw it out when it gets dirty.

2

Use It For The Move

Moving out soon? Keep your boxes for it! You won't have to get new ones and they help you keep things organized too!

3

Checking For Leaks

Suspect a leak from your car or house? Place some flattened cardboard on the area and check back in a few hours!

Soaks & stains on cardboard are highly visible, and it saves your floor from getting dirty!

Retire it when: it gets stained, soaked or is starting to peel apart.
