SECTION 2 - QUICK ALTERNATIVE

When You're On The Go

No sink nearby? You can still make a responsible disposal decision in a few quick steps.

1

Empty The Bottle Or Hold Onto It

Look around and try to find a drinking water fountain with a drain. Rinse if you can find one.

Consider if you can hold onto the bottle until you can find a sink or drinking water fountain.

If you cannot do either, then throw it in the trash.

2

Pour Out & Shake

Pour away as much rinse water as you can.

Give it a few shakes to shake out as much as you can, then screw the cap back on.

3

Recycling Time

If only droplets remain, feel free to throw it in the recycling bin.

Make sure the cap is screwed on tightly to prevent small leaks if still wet.

Important: Not every recycling bin accepts every plastic type. Check the bin label and your building or city's rules. Do not place food/liquid-filled bottles in recycling.

Source: Department Of Ecology, State of Washington - Recycle Right

SECTION 4 - REDUCE

Use Fewer Single-Use Bottles

The best bottle to recycle is the one you never had to throw out.

1

Buy Your Own Reusable Bottle

Use and reuse your own washable bottle made of more durable plastic or stainless steel. Its a one-time cost and saves you more money in the long run too!

2

Buy The Bigger Option

If you're getting milk or juice for daily consumption, get the bigger bottle options. This reduces your plastic per gallon use over the long run.

3

Try Glass Where You Can

Pick glass bottles when you have the option. They are more durable as long as you are careful in handling (dont shatter it), and can be reused more.

Reduce comes first: every one-time bottle you avoid is one you don't have to clean, sort, or find a bin for.

Source: EPA - What You Can Do To Reduce Plastic Waste

SECTION 5 - REUSE

Give Your Bottle A Second Life

A sturdy bottle that is still in good shape can be used again before it is recycled.

1

Reuse Water Bottles To Store Water

You can always reuse your plastic water bottle to store water. Refill it at home and chill it in the fridge for a refreshing sip! Throw it out after a few times of reuse!

You must only use water bottles to store water. Do Not Use Others That Held Other Drinks Or Chemicals!

Make sure you wash it each refill to prevent bacteria buildup!

2

Use It For The Plants

Reuse your bottle to water your plants or as temporary pots! Its easy and flexible since you can cut them into shape with only scissors!

3

Household Organizing & DIY

If it is still in good shape, cut it in half and file any sharp edges away. You can reuse these as temporary cups to hold everyday items like plastic grocery bags or for DIY activities.

Retire it when: it cracks, warps, or stays stained or smelly. Then follow the steps in Section 1 and recycle it.

Source: EPA - What You Can Do To Reduce Plastic Waste
