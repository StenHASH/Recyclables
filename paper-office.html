SECTION 2 - QUICK ALTERNATIVE

When You're On The Go

Carrying papers between classes? You can still make a responsible disposal decision in a few quick steps.

1

Keep It Dry & Flat

Slide loose pages into a folder or notebook so they stay flat and dry in your bag.

Paper that gets wet or soiled can't be recycled.

2

Look Around For A Paper Bin

Take a look around for a bin marked for paper. If you spot one, go ahead and recycle your pages!

Check the bin label first. A bin marked only for cans and bottles is not a paper bin.

3

Hold Onto It Or Not?

If you can't find a paper bin, decide if your pages are light enough to carry until you do.

If it is too much of a hassle, or the paper is wet or soiled, find the nearest trash bin and dispose of it there.

Important: Napkins, tissues, paper towels and used coffee cups do not go in paper recycling. Rules for shredded paper vary, so ask your building or recycling program before recycling it.

Source: UIC - Recyclable Materials

SECTION 4 - REDUCE

Use Less Paper

The easiest paper to recycle is the sheet you never printed.

1

Print Double-Sided

Change your printer settings to double-sided, so one sheet does the work of two.

2

Go Digital

Scan or email documents instead of printing them, and read on-screen when you can. Only print what you really need.

3

Fit More On A Page

Print several slides or pages per sheet in your printer's Print Properties, and delete stray blank pages before you print.

Reduce comes first: every page you don't print is one you don't have to sort, carry, or find a bin for.

Source: King County - Reducing Office Paper Consumption

SECTION 5 - REUSE

Give Paper A Second Life

A sheet with one clean side still has a use.

1

Use The Blank Side

Use paper that is blank on one side for notes, drafts, scratch work or study practice.

2

Make Scrap Notepads

Cut spare sheets into quarters and staple them together for quick lists and reminders.

3

Donate Unused Paper & Notebooks

UIC's Great Stuff Exchange accepts clean notebooks, paper, envelopes and binders, and passes them on to students, staff and faculty for free.

Email recycling@uic.edu or see the link below to donate.

Retire it when: both sides are used. Then follow the steps in Section 1 and recycle it. Paper that is soiled or soaked goes in the trash.
