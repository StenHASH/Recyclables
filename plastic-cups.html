SECTION 2 - QUICK ALTERNATIVE

When You're On The Go

Finished your drink and don't have a sink nearby? Here's how to make a quick disposal decision.

1

Empty Your Cup

Finish your drink or empty any leftover liquid and ice into an appropriate drain. Do not pour drinks into a recycling bin.

2

Separate The Straw

Remove the straw and dispose of it in the trash.

Separate the lid and check your local program's rules before recycling it.

3

Make The Bin Decision

In Chicago, disposable plastic cups belong in the regular trash, not the Blue Cart.

If you're elsewhere, check the bin label or local recycling guidance before deciding.

Important: An empty or clean plastic cup is not automatically recyclable. Chicago's Blue Cart program does not accept disposable plastic cups.

Source: Recycle by City - Chicago Plastic Cups

SECTION 4 - REDUCE

Use Fewer Single-Use Plastic Cups

Reducing disposable cup usage prevents waste before it is created.

1

Bring Your Own Reusable Cup

Carry a washable cup or tumbler when getting coffee, water, or other drinks.

Ask whether the business allows customers to bring their own cups.

2

Skip Unnecessary Extras

If you don't need a straw or lid, ask for your drink without them.

Avoid taking extra disposable cups when one is enough.

3

Use Refillable Drink Containers

Refill your own bottle or cup instead of choosing a new disposable cup for every drink.

This can reduce plastic waste throughout the day.

Reduce comes first: Every disposable cup you avoid is one less item that needs to be discarded.

Source: EPA - What You Can Do To Reduce Plastic Waste

SECTION 5 - REUSE

Give Your Plastic Cup A Second Life

A clean disposable cup can sometimes be repurposed before being discarded.

1

Use It For Small Plants

A clean cup can be repurposed as a temporary seed-starting container.

Make drainage holes carefully and transfer the plant to a larger pot when it grows.

2

Store Small Household Items

Wash and dry the cup, then use it to hold pens, pencils, paper clips, or other small supplies.

Keep lightweight items inside to avoid tipping it over.

3

Use It For Arts & Crafts

Repurpose a clean cup to hold paintbrushes, craft supplies, or small materials during projects.

Avoid exposing disposable cups to high heat or using damaged cups for food and drinks.

Retire it when: The cup becomes cracked, damaged, or difficult to clean. In Chicago, disposable plastic cups belong in the regular trash.

Source: EPA - What You Can Do To Reduce Plastic Waste
